Our CAC tripled in Q3. What should we do?
Before I speculate, three quick questions:

1. Where in the funnel is CAC growing — click cost, landing page CVR, or trial-to-paid?
2. Has your creative refresh cadence changed, or same ads since January?
3. Has your ICP targeting or channel mix shifted?

Pirate Metrics Funnel diagnosis
CTR is fine, but trial-to-paid dropped from 18% to 9%. Same creatives since January.
That's a product-market fit signal, not a CAC problem. Ad fatigue is attracting lower-intent users now. The real fix is in onboarding and activation — not media spend. Here's what I'd do this week…
